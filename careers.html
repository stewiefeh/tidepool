Careers

The caseload cap is the job description.

We cap caseloads at two-thirds of industry norms, pay above market, and bill for forty-five minutes of your attention, not your evenings. Zero turnover in three years is the proof, not the promise.

What working here is like

  • Caseload of 40–53 active kids, never 70+
  • Admin time is scheduled and paid, not squeezed into lunch
  • One shared chart across disciplines; no re-entering your own notes into three systems
  • Annual CEU budget that actually covers conference travel every other year
  • Supervision and mentorship hours on the clock for CFs and new grads
  • Four-day week option at 0.8 FTE with benefits

Compensation: salaried, published bands by role and licensure, posted in every job listing. We don't do "competitive salary" as a way of hiding a number.
