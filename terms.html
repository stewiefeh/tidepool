Using this site means agreeing to the sensible terms below. They exist mostly to keep the fiction honest.

What this site is

This is a fictional portfolio website. Tidepool Pediatric Therapy, its clinicians, address, phone number, credentials, outcomes, and rates are invented. No real medical practice is described, and no real patients or families appear here.

No medical advice

Nothing on this site is medical advice, and nothing here establishes a clinician-patient relationship, because no such relationship is possible with a portfolio piece. The resource guides reflect real, publicly documented clinical principles but cannot account for any real child's situation. For concerns about a real child, speak with a licensed pediatric professional.

Do not misrepresent this site

You may not present this site as a real clinic, quote its fictional rates or outcomes as real, or use its contact details for any real-world solicitation. Reviewers, recruiters, and evaluators: everything here was authored as design work, and that framing should travel with any excerpt.

Permitted use

You may view, share, and link to this site freely. Educational discussion of its design is welcome. You may not copy its code or content into a product, or present it as your own work.
