Getting started

Four steps from "I'm worried" to a plan

No referral required for a screening. No eleven-page intake packet before we ever meet you. Here is exactly how it works.

Free 20-minute consult

A phone or video call with a clinician, not a call center. You describe what you're seeing; we tell you honestly whether an evaluation is needed.

Evaluation visit

A 60-minute play-based session. We use standardized measures where they matter and observation everywhere else. You get a written report within five business days.

Your child's plan

Goals written with you, not for you. Frequency, duration, and what progress looks like in four weeks, in plain language.

Therapy, then transition

Weekly or twice-weekly sessions, re-checked every twelve weeks. The end goal is always the same: your child not needing us.

Start with step one

We believe the waiting list is part of the problem.

The average wait for pediatric therapy in our county is four to seven months. Ours is two weeks. We hold clinician caseloads at two-thirds of industry norms, hire ahead of demand, and would rather say "our speech team is full, here's exactly when we can start" than park your child on a list and hope you forget about it.
