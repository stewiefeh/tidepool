♿ Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Built to WCAG 2.2 AA on purpose, and measured against it, not just claimed.

What that means here

  • Contrast: all text is deep ink-teal on warm light surfaces, checked at AA for body text and AAA for headings. No low-contrast gray-on-gray anywhere.
  • Keyboard: every interactive element is reachable and operable by keyboard, with a visible focus ring that never disappears. A skip link jumps straight to content.
  • Motion: all animation respects the system reduced-motion setting. With it enabled, reveals, drifts, and the breathing pool are neutralized instantly.
  • Structure: one h1 per page, landmarks for header, main, and footer, form fields with real labels, and error states that are announced rather than colored only.
  • Zoom: layouts hold at 200% zoom and reflow to single column; no horizontal scrolling is ever required to read text.

Known limits

The decorative tidepool illustration is aria-hidden and conveys nothing required. The tide clock is a playful detail that duplicates the time, so missing it costs nothing. If you use a screen reader and find a gap we missed, tell us; accessibility fixes at this site rank above every feature request.

Statement reviewed October 15, 2026 against WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
