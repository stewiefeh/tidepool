FAQ

Questions parents actually ask.

Grouped by what's on your mind. If your question isn't here, ask us directly; the answer becomes a new entry.

The process

Do we need a pediatrician referral? +

No. California is a direct-access state for therapy evaluations. Some insurance plans do require a physician referral for reimbursement; our benefits check tells you before anything is scheduled, and we help you get the paperwork moving the same week.

How fast can we start? +

Median wait from first call to first visit is eleven days. Speech runs fullest (two weeks is common there); PT often has room within days. If a discipline is full, we tell you the real number and hold nothing hostage.

What happens at the evaluation? +

A 60-minute play-based session with standardized measures where they matter. Parents stay in the room. You leave with our initial read the same day and a written report within five business days.

Will my child always see the same therapist? +

Yes, by design. Rapport is the first intervention. Our clinician turnover in the last three years is zero, and if that ever changes mid-plan, you get two weeks of overlapping handoff sessions, not a surprise phone call.

Money & insurance

Do you take my insurance? +

We're in-network with nine plans (full list on the insurance page). Out of network, we provide a superbill you can submit for reimbursement, and we'll tell you honestly what the likely recovery rate is before you spend a dollar.

What will this actually cost us? +

After the benefits check you get a written estimate: evaluation cost, per-session cost, and your expected annual total at the recommended frequency. No surprise line items. The full rate table is public on the insurance page.

Do you offer a sliding scale? +

Yes. Four reduced-rate slots are reserved at all times, no documentation gauntlet, first-come. Ask for it by name; nobody here will make you justify your finances.

Therapy itself

How long until we see progress? +

Plan reviews happen every twelve weeks with the progress graph on the table. Many families see meaningful change inside eight, but we won't promise a timeline we don't control. What we control is measurement: if the line is flat, the plan changes.

Can I watch sessions? +

Not just can: should. Every room has an observation window, and every plan includes structured parent coaching. A therapy model that hides the method from the family can't work at home, so we don't use it.

My child has a diagnosis. Does that change anything? +

It shapes the plan, not the welcome. We work with autistic children, kids with Down syndrome, cerebral palsy, prematurity histories, and every undiagnosed worry that brings a family through the door. The evaluation tells us where to start; the child tells us the rest.

What ages do you serve? +

Birth through eighteen, including Early Start referrals for infants and toddlers. Our youngest clients come in for milestone checks; our oldest for return-to-play programs after injuries.
