About the clinic

Built around the wait, not the waitlist.

Tidepool opened in 2016 because our founder, Marisol Vance, was tired of telling worried parents "four to six months." Everything about the clinic, the caseload caps, the shared chart, the kitchen suite, traces back to that one sentence she kept saying.

The caseload math

The industry norm for a pediatric therapist is a caseload of 60 to 80 children. Our cap is two-thirds of that: 40 to 53. It costs us revenue every month and buys us three things: clinicians who stay (zero turnover in three years), sessions that start on time, and the two-week wait.

When a discipline fills up, we say so on this site and on the phone. We hire ahead of demand rather than rationing access to it. Speech is currently our fullest schedule; PT usually has room within days.

One chart, really one

Most "multidisciplinary" clinics run four schedules in four systems and a phone tree between them. Ours runs one. When your child's SLP notices a sensory regulation pattern mid-session, your OT sees the note the same afternoon, not after the next case conference.

The kitchen suite

Our feeding program's best tool is a working kitchen with a family table. Real meals, real siblings when they come along, real cleanup. Therapy that only survives in a clinic room isn't therapy; it's performance.

The harbor, room by room

  • The sensory gym: rock wall, two swings, mats rated for exactly the crashes kids attempt
  • Two fine-motor studios with adjustable-height tables for wheelchair users
  • The regulation room: dimmed lights, soft floor, zero demands until the child is ready
  • The kitchen suite for feeding therapy and real family meals
  • Four treatment rooms, one observation window each, coffee for parents

By the numbers

  • Founded 2016, locally owned, no private equity
  • 1,240+ evaluations completed since opening
  • 96% of families return or graduate from care
  • 11-day median wait to first visit, county-wide median: 19 weeks

Our promise, on the record

We will tell you honestly when your child needs therapy and honestly when they don't. We will never hold a spot hostage to urgency you don't feel. We will show you the progress graph, not just describe it. And we will work toward your child not needing us, because that is the only ending worth billing for.
