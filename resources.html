Santa Cruz County services that return calls

  • San Andreas Regional Center — Early Start intake for children under three. Assessment in about three weeks, and it is free regardless of income or immigration status.
  • First 5 Santa Cruz — free developmental screening events monthly; ask for the "Ages & Stages" clinic.
  • SCUSD special education intake — if your child is three or older, request an assessment in writing; the district has 60 days to respond by law.
  • Parents' place (family resource center) — parent-to-parent matching, Wednesday drop-ins, and the best playground gossip network in the county.

Our lending library

Twenty AAC devices, sensory kits, feeding chairs, and about two hundred books in the lobby, free to borrow for any family in the county, client or not. Ask at the front desk; there is no paperwork gauntlet and no late fee.

Waiting is hard. Two weeks is not nothing, but it is not forever.

If you are on any clinic's waitlist right now: request the screening consult anyway. It is usually free, it usually happens faster than the evaluation queue, and it usually moves your place in line.
