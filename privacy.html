🔒 Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: this is a portfolio website. We collect nothing, track nothing, and store nothing. The long version is still short.

What this site collects

Nothing. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no ad scripts, no session recording, no fonts loaded from third parties that would phone home. Every asset on this site, including the two typefaces, is served from this domain.

The demo forms

The consult request, contact, and newsletter forms are demonstrations. When you submit one, the site validates the fields locally, shows a confirmation message that says it's a demo, and discards everything. Nothing is transmitted to any server, and no personal data is stored anywhere by us.

Children's information

Because the fictional clinic this site depicts serves children, we say it plainly anyway: if a real site operated this way, it would not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13, in line with COPPA, and this portfolio site collects nothing from anyone of any age.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare's standard CDN handling applies to page requests, as it does to every site on the platform. We have added no analytics, marketing, or advertising integrations of any kind.

Changes

If this page ever changes, the change will be dated at the bottom of this page. As of the date below, the policy is: nothing collected, nothing stored, nothing shared.
