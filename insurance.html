Real numbers, before you spend a dollar.

We call your insurance while you wait and put the estimate in writing. The full rate table is public, because a family deciding about their child's therapy deserves to see the price before the phone call, not after.

Out of network

We provide itemized superbills for any PPO plan. Before you commit, we will tell you our honest estimate of what your specific plan is likely to reimburse, based on the last dozen families who submitted to it. If the answer is "not much," we say so and talk about the self-pay path instead.

The sliding scale

Four reduced-rate slots are reserved at all times, first-come, no documentation gauntlet. Ask for it by name at the consult. Nobody here will make you justify your finances to get your child seen.

What we don't do

We don't run credit checks, don't charge no-show fees for cancellations made more than four hours ahead, and don't auto-charge a card on file. If a family's card declines, Wren calls and works out a plan before a session is ever cancelled over it.

The benefits check is free and happens before anything is scheduled.

Give Wren your member ID and ten minutes. You get back: whether we're in-network for you, your exact copays, any visit limits your plan applies, and what the out-of-network route would look like if we're not. In writing. Then you decide.
