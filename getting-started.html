Getting started

Twenty minutes to an honest answer.

What happens next

The four steps

Free consult (20 min)

Phone or video, your choice. A clinician listens and gives you an honest read.

Benefits check (same day)

We call your insurance while you wait and tell you exactly what evaluation and sessions would cost you.

Evaluation (60 min)

Play-based, standardized where it counts. Written report within five business days.

First therapy week

Usually within eleven days of the evaluation. Same clinician every visit from here on.

Before you call anyone

Questions worth asking any clinic

Even if you don't choose us, ask these:

  • What is the actual wait for a first evaluation, in weeks?
  • Will my child see the same clinician every visit? What happens when they leave?
  • How many children are on my therapist's caseload?
  • Do you share notes across disciplines, and how fast?
  • What does my family pay after insurance, in dollars, for evaluation and for each session?

Any clinic that answers all five in specific numbers is a reasonable choice. The ones that don't have a reason.
